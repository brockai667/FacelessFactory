## FAQ
**Do you need footage or assets from me?** No. Your website is enough. I take your logo, colors, fonts and screens from it.
**Can you use my app screens if it's behind a login?** Yes, send screenshots or a screen recording.
**Is the music licensed?** Yes, royalty-free or CC BY with credit, included.
**Can I get it without music for my own edit?** Yes, overlays on green/black are in Premium or as an extra.
